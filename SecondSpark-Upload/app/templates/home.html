Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about uploading and reviving projects on SecondSpark.

What kind of projects can I post on SecondSpark?
Any project that is stalled, incomplete, or broken! This includes hardware prototypes (Arduino, Raspberry Pi, ESP32, robotics), web & mobile applications, AI/ML models, IoT systems, mechanical CAD designs, and college capstones.
Is it free to list and collaborate on projects?
Yes! Listing projects and contacting creators on SecondSpark is completely free. Project owners can also define compensation/budgets if they are looking for paid specialized assistance.
How do I protect my intellectual property?
You can share high-level summaries publicly while keeping proprietary schematics, CAD files, and API keys private until you establish a direct conversation and NDA agreement with your collaborator.
How do I communicate with project owners?
Simply click "Offer Help" or "Contact Owner" on any project details page to launch a direct real-time conversation thread within the SecondSpark messaging portal.
